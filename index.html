1. Abstract & Vision

Digital Dinar (DDN) is a decentralized monetary system designed to resolve the central failure of modern fiat currency: the arbitrary expansion of supply through interest-bearing debt.

Modern central banking permits money to be created through lending at interest. This mechanism produces systemic inflation, transfers wealth from savers to issuers, and violates the ethical prohibition of Riba — interest — which is central to Islamic finance and to classical theories of just money.

A monetary system with no interest, no arbitrary issuance, and no central authority capable of devaluing savings.

Inspired by the scarcity model of Bitcoin, Digital Dinar establishes a total supply of 100,000,000 DDN, fixed in perpetuity.

Vision Statement: To establish a global, Halal, decentralized currency that preserves purchasing power across generations — enabling participants to mine seamlessly and utilize DDN for everyday purchases, daily living expenses, and lifelong wealth preservation.

2. Ethical & Economic Framework

2.1 Prohibition of Riba: No lending protocol issues DDN at interest, no staking yield from debt, no inflationary reward funded by debasing holders.

2.2 Arbitrary Inflation: Fiat expands at will. DDN addresses this through absolute scarcity with a public, auditable, immutable schedule.

2.3 Finite Utility Drives Value: Scarcity, divisibility to 8 decimals, durability, portability, verifiability, and ethical utility.
