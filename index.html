Version: 1.0 | Author: ukinako | Date: 2026 | Symbol: DDN | Status: Live

1. Abstract: Digital Dinar is a fixed-supply monetary system designed to eliminate interest-based inflation. Inspired by the scarcity model of Bitcoin, Digital Dinar establishes a total supply of 100,000,000 DDN, fixed in perpetuity.

4. Security Architecture: SHA-256 Cryptographic Hashing. Merkle Tree Verification. Each Block Is Immutable And Publicly Auditable.

5. Ethical Framework: No Interest. No Riba. No Arbitrary Issuance. Value Is Derived From Scarcity, Utility, and Market Consensus.

6. Vision: A Global, Halal, Decentralized Currency. One DDN Represents A Stable Unit of Purchasing Power.
